F-002 HIGH CERTAIN OPEN standards · L9
Investments at fair value · p. 3 · Investments at fair value

The Statement of Assets and Liabilities omits the parallel cost column for investments. ASC 946-210-45-1 requires both cost and fair value when the fund holds investments measured at fair value.

ASC 946-210-45-1
“Investments at fair value $ 2,184,500,000”
Recommended fixAdd a 'Cost' column adjacent to 'Fair value' for investments, with the corresponding aggregate cost figure tied to the Schedule of Investments.

F-003 HIGH CERTAIN OPEN mechanical · L2
Realized and unrealized gain/loss · p. 4 · Net realized and unrealized gain/loss

Realized vs unrealized gain/loss on investments is combined into a single line ($142.3M). Bifurcation is required and available — split into Realized $61.2M and Unrealized $81.1M.

SOO!C26 = 61,200,000 ; SOO!C27 = 81,100,000 ; sum ties to SOO!C28 = 142,300,000

F-011 CRITICAL PROBABLE OPEN mechanical · L8
Level 3 designation · p. 13 · Project Titan Holdings

Hidden workbook row at SOI!47 classifies Project Titan Holdings as Level 2. Visible row at SOI!46 (same investment) shows Level 3. The published FS shows Level 3. The hidden row may indicate either a working-paper override or an unresolved valuation question.

“Project Titan Holdings — Level 3 — $ 87,400,000”
SOI!K46 = 'Level 3' (visible) ; SOI!K47 = 'Level 2' (hidden, same investment)

F-005 HIGH CERTAIN ACCEPTED standards · L9
Concentration · p. 12 · Other private equity investments (aggregated)

The Schedule of Investments aggregates $158M (6.3% of NAV) under 'Other private equity investments' without name-level disclosure. ASC 946-210-50-6 requires name-level disclosure of any investment >5% of net assets.

ASC 946-210-50-6
“Other private equity investments $ 158,000,000”
Recommended fixBreak out the constituent investments within the aggregated line by name; verify any single name >5% of NAV is separately disclosed.

Notes to Financial Statements

8 findings 2 C3 H3 M

Disclosure infrastructure. Significant accounting policies through subsequent events.

F-013 HIGH CERTAIN OPEN narrative · L7
Significant accounting policies · Note 2 · p. 15

The Significant Accounting Policies note references 'the Fund's investments in AAA Coinvest Fund III portfolio companies' — AAA Coinvest Fund III is a different vehicle. This appears to be carried-over language from a prior draft of a different fund's FS.

“Investments are recorded at fair value, including the Fund's investments in AAA Coinvest Fund III portfolio companies.”
Recommended fixReplace 'AAA Coinvest Fund III' with the correct entity name ('AAA Coinvest Fund A LP' or 'the Fund'). Scan the entire note set for additional cross-fund residue.
F-004 CRITICAL CERTAIN OPEN standards · L9
Fair value measurements · Note 4(b) · p. 17

The Level 3 reconciliation roll-forward in Note 4(b) presents opening balance, purchases, and ending balance. ASC 820-10-50-2(c) requires opening balance, purchases, sales, settlements, issuances, transfers in/out, realized gain/loss, unrealized gain/loss, and ending balance. The still-held subset of realized/unrealized per -2(d) is also absent.

ASC 820-10-50-2c
“Opening balance $ 387,000,000 / Purchases $ 25,400,000 / Ending balance $ 412,400,000”
Recommended fixExpand the Level 3 roll-forward to include all eight required movement columns and the still-held subset disclosure. Verify the resulting ending balance ties to the Schedule of Investments Level 3 subtotal ($412.4M).
F-004a CRITICAL CERTAIN OPEN mechanical · L2
Fair value measurements · Note 4(b) · p. 17

Level 3 reconciliation ending balance ($412.4M) does not foot to the Schedule of Investments Level 3 subtotal ($408.9M); break $3.5M.

Recommended fixSee root cause F-004 — this constituent was decoupled by the controller and now stands alone.
F-006 MEDIUM CERTAIN OPEN defense · L12
Organization · Note 1 · p. 14

The Organization note does not reference Cayman Private Funds Act registration. The Fund is a Cayman exempted limited partnership formed post-2020 — PFA registration disclosure is the standard expectation.

CIMA:PFA
“AAA Coinvest Fund A LP (the 'Fund') is a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership formed on [date].”
Recommended fixAdd a sentence to the Organization note: 'The Fund is registered with the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority under the Private Funds Act (registration number [X]).' Verify the registration number from formation documents.
F-007 HIGH PROBABLE OPEN defense · L14
Related party transactions · Note 8 · p. 22

The Related Party Transactions note discloses the General Partner and Investment Manager relationships but does not disclose the Fund's relationship to AAA Portfolio Operations LLC, which is named in the Schedule of Investments as the operating-services counterparty for two portfolio companies. The auditor will likely push back on this.

“The Fund has entered into a management agreement with the Investment Manager...”
Recommended fixConfirm whether AAA Portfolio Operations LLC is an affiliate of the GP/Manager. If yes, expand Note 8 to disclose: nature of relationship, terms of services, period amount, and balance receivable/payable at period end.
F-008 HIGH PROBABLE OPEN defense · L12
Income taxes · Note 9 · p. 25

The Income Taxes note states the Fund has no uncertain tax positions. The Schedule of Investments shows non-US corporate investments. The note does not address PFIC treatment for US investors. If any non-US corporate investment qualifies as a PFIC, the disclosure regime under IRC §1297–1298 applies.

IRS:PFIC
“The Fund has no uncertain tax positions as of the period end.”
Recommended fixConfirm with tax counsel which non-US corporate investments qualify as PFICs. Add disclosure covering: (a) presence of PFIC investments, (b) treatment (mark-to-market under §1296 / QEF under §1295 / default §1291), (c) whether elections have been made on investors' behalf.
F-009 MEDIUM PROBABLE OPEN standards · L9
Recently issued accounting standards · Note 13 · p. 30

The 'Recently issued accounting standards' note mentions ASU 2022-03 but does not address ASU 2023-09 (income tax disclosures, effective FY 2025+) — which may be applicable if the Fund has taxable blocker subsidiaries.

ASC 105-10-65
“The Fund adopted ASU 2022-03 (Equity Securities with Contractual Sale Restrictions) effective January 1, 2024.”
Recommended fixConfirm with tax team whether ASU 2023-09 applies (taxable blocker subsidiaries with rate reconciliation). If applicable, add the ASU 2023-09 effective-date and expected-effect disclosure to Note 13.
F-010 MEDIUM CERTAIN OPEN narrative · L3
Subsequent events · Note 14 · p. 31

The Subsequent Events note states events were evaluated through April 15, 2026. The draft is dated May 17, 2026 — the evaluation date precedes the draft date by over a month. Either the evaluation date is stale or the draft date is pre-dated.

ASC 855-10-50-4
“The Fund has evaluated subsequent events through April 15, 2026.”
Recommended fixUpdate the subsequent-events evaluation date to the expected issuance date. Re-evaluate events occurring between the prior date and the new date.

F-012 HIGH CERTAIN OPEN comparative · L11
Carried interest reconciliation · Carried interest accrual

AAA Coinvest Fund A LP records carried interest accrual of $34.2M on the Statement of Changes. The sibling AIV vehicle (AAA Coinvest Fund A AIV LP) FS records zero carried interest accrual. Per the LPA, the AIV applies a parallel waterfall — a proportional accrual is expected.

“Carried interest accrual $ 34,200,000”
Prior / sibling: “AIV FS Note 7: 'No carried interest has been allocated as of period end.'”
Recommended fixReconcile the carried interest accrual between Fund A and Fund A AIV. Either correct one of them or document the timing/policy reason for the difference in both sets of FS.
