# CSSF and Luxembourg: SHINE v9 Regulatory Corpus

**Version:** 9.0.0
**Attestation:** 2026-06-12 (steward refresh window 180 days)
**Scope:** Luxembourg vehicles in the fund complex (SARLs and supervised fund vehicles).

## CSSF:SUPERVISION

Luxembourg regulated vehicles operate under the supervision of the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier. Annual accounts are filed with the Registre de Commerce et des Societes (RCS). The organization note of a supervised Luxembourg vehicle is expected to reference the supervisory regime and the filing obligation.

## CSSF:02-77

CSSF Circular 02/77 governs the treatment of NAV calculation errors and breaches of investment rules for supervised funds: materiality thresholds by fund type, the correction process, and investor compensation. Valuation governance disclosures should be consistent with the circular's framework where applicable.

## LUX:RCS-FILING

Luxembourg companies law requires annual accounts to be filed with the RCS within seven months of year end. A vehicle whose statements are dated near the deadline should evidence the filing timeline.
