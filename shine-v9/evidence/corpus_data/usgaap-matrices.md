## ASC 230: Statement of Cash Flows

- **ASC 230-10-15-1** : A statement of cash flows is required as part of a complete set of financial statements for all business entities, presenting cash receipts and payments classified by operating, investing, and financing activities.

## ASC 850: Related Party Disclosures

- **ASC 850-10-50-1** : Financial statements include disclosures of material related party transactions: the nature of the relationship, a description of the transactions, the dollar amounts, and amounts due to or from the related parties at the balance sheet date.
