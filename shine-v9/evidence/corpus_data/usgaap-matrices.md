# US GAAP Supplement: SHINE v9 Authority Corpus

**Version:** 9.0.0
**Attestation:** 2026-06-12
**Purpose:** ASC topics required by the US GAAP framework engine that sit outside the v8 investment-company matrix. ASC 820, 740 and other shared topics are indexed from the v8 corpus directly.

## ASC 230: Statement of Cash Flows

- **ASC 230-10-15-1** : A statement of cash flows is required as part of a complete set of financial statements for all business entities, presenting cash receipts and payments classified by operating, investing, and financing activities.

## ASC 850: Related Party Disclosures

- **ASC 850-10-50-1** : Financial statements include disclosures of material related party transactions: the nature of the relationship, a description of the transactions, the dollar amounts, and amounts due to or from the related parties at the balance sheet date.

## ASC 205-30: Liquidation Basis of Accounting

- **ASC 205-30-25-1** : When liquidation is imminent, financial statements are prepared on the liquidation basis of accounting: assets at expected cash proceeds, liabilities at expected settlement amounts, and the basis change disclosed prominently in the accounting policies.

## ASC 250: Accounting Changes and Error Corrections

- **ASC 250-10-50-1** : A change in accounting principle requires disclosure of the nature of and reason for the change, the method of applying it, and the effect on the financial statements of the period of change, including retrospective application where required.
