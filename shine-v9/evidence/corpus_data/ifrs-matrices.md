## IAS 1: Presentation of Financial Statements

- **IAS 1.10** : A complete set of financial statements comprises a statement of financial position, a statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income, a statement of changes in equity, a statement of cash flows, and notes. There is no election out of the cash flow statement under IFRS.

## IFRS 10: Consolidated Financial Statements (Investment Entities)

- **IFRS 10.27** : An investment entity measures its investments in subsidiaries at fair value through profit or loss rather than consolidating them. The investment entity conclusion and its basis must be evident from the accounting policies.

## IFRS 12: Disclosure of Interests in Other Entities

- **IFRS 12.19** : An investment entity discloses information about its interests in unconsolidated subsidiaries and structured entities, including the nature and extent of restrictions and support arrangements.

## IFRS 13: Fair Value Measurement

- **IFRS 13.93** : Quantitative disclosures for fair value measurements categorised within the hierarchy: levels, transfers between levels, and valuation techniques and inputs for Level 2 and Level 3 measurements.
- **IFRS 13.93(e)** : For recurring Level 3 measurements, a reconciliation from opening to closing balances disclosing gains and losses, purchases, sales, issues, settlements, and transfers into or out of Level 3.

## IFRS 7: Financial Instruments Disclosures

- **IFRS 7.31** : An entity discloses information that enables users to evaluate the nature and extent of risks arising from financial instruments: credit risk, liquidity risk, and market risk, including sensitivity analysis.

## IAS 24: Related Party Disclosures

- **IAS 24.18** : Disclosure of related party transactions: the nature of the relationship, the amount of the transactions, and outstanding balances including commitments.
