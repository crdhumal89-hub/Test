# Delaware — DRULPA / DLLCA Regulatory Corpus

**Last attestation:** 2026-05-17
**Attestation age policy:** ≤180 days.
**Consumed by:** `subagents/defense/SKILL.md` (L12 regulatory compliance)

Delaware statutory framework applicable to Delaware-domiciled limited partnerships and LLCs. Most Apollo funds are either Cayman or Delaware; this corpus governs the Delaware ones.

---

## DE:DRULPA-17-302 — Limited Partner Voting and Consent

**Scope:** Delaware limited partnerships.

**Requirement:** Limited partners may vote on matters specified in the LPA without being deemed to participate in management (limited liability preserved).

**FS disclosure expectations:** Generally not a direct FS note; cross-referenced when LPA-driven voting on advisory committee decisions affects allocations or governance disclosed in the FS.

---

## DE:DRULPA-17-403 — General Partner Duties

**Scope:** Delaware LPs.

**Requirement:** GP duties to the partnership and limited partners; can be modified by LPA (with exceptions for the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing).

**FS disclosure expectations:** Related-party note typically describes GP role and compensation. SHINE cross-references this section when the related-party narrative is thin.

---

## DE:DRULPA-17-1101 — Contractual Nature; Permitted Modifications to Fiduciary Duties

**Scope:** Delaware LPs.

**Requirement:** Permits LPAs to expand, restrict, or eliminate fiduciary duties (but NOT the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing).

**FS disclosure expectations:** Where the FS related-party note mentions waivers of GP fiduciary duties, this section is the regulatory anchor.

**SHINE detection cues (L12):**
- Related-party note references GP fiduciary modifications without citing the LPA or statutory basis.

---

## DE:DRULPA-17-1110 — Indemnification and Exculpation

**Scope:** Delaware LPs.

**Requirement:** Permits LPA to provide for indemnification and exculpation of GP and affiliates (subject to public policy limits).

**FS disclosure expectations:** Commitments and contingencies note often references GP indemnification; SHINE cross-references when the contingency disclosure is boilerplate.

---

## DE:DLLCA-18-215 — Series LLCs

**Scope:** Delaware LLCs operated as series LLCs (each series has separate assets and liabilities).

**Requirement:** Series-level liability segregation requires (a) LLC agreement provides for series, (b) records maintained for each series, (c) public notice of series.

**FS disclosure expectations:** Series-LLC funds disclose the series structure and the liability segregation; each series typically files its own FS.

**SHINE detection cues:**
- Series-LLC fund where the FS combines series without disclosure of segregation.
