    Look-Through Pricing & P&L · active product SPORT
    Bottom-up NAV repricing. The deepest (terminal) funds price at NAV ÷ units; each holder's Revised MV = Σ held-qty × child revised price + its direct securities, propagated to the product. Publish price = NAV ÷ units (emphasised column) is the daily unit price to send. Repricing P&L = Revised − Derived at each level, and per holding = held-qty × (revised px − current px). Sort any column; click a fund for its per-holding P&L and who-holds drill.
    Repricing Walk — bottom-up, deepest level first → product last. Each fund's unit price = value ÷ global quantity. Before = derived look-through at current position marks; After = revised value, underlyings NAV-repriced bottom-up. Δ Value (P&L) = Value after − Value before = each level's repricing P&L. Click any column header to sort.
